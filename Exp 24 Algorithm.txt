Backward Chaining Algorithm: 
	1. Define the available facts and rules.
	2. Select a goal or conclusion to prove.
	3. Check whether the goal is already a known fact.
	4. If not, find a rule whose conclusion matches the goal.
	5. Treat the rule's conditions as new sub-goals.
	6. Recursively check whether all sub-goals can be proved.
	7. If all conditions are satisfied, the goal is proved.
	8. Display the final diagnosis.
