Crypt Arithmetic Algorithm
	1. Define the words involved in the crypt arithmetic problem.
	2. Assign a unique digit (0–9) to each letter.
	3. Ensure no two letters have the same digit.
	4. Ensure the leading letter of each word is not assigned 0.
	5. Check whether the arithmetic equation is satisfied.
	6. If satisfied, display the digit assignment; otherwise, continue searching.
	7. Stop when a valid solution is found.
