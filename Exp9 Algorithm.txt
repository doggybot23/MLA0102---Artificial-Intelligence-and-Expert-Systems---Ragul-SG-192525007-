Water Jug Algorithm
	1. Start with both jugs empty.
	2. Store the initial state (0, 0) in a queue.
	3. Mark visited states to avoid repetition.
	4. Generate all possible next states:
		a. Fill Jug 1.
		b.Fill Jug 2.
		c. Empty Jug 1.
		d. Empty Jug 2.
		e. Pour water from Jug 1 to Jug 
		f. Pour water from Jug 2 to Jug 1.
	5. Repeat until the target quantity is found in either jug.
	6. Print the sequence of states from the initial state to the goal state.
